## Chosen shape

Keep one human-facing skill with a small operational interface: enter at the existing work, establish or recover the agreement, choose useful work, realize and inspect it, seek independent criticism at consequential decisions, then preserve and decide.

The brief is the working agreement. One active record holds or links current facts. The graph carries relationships, not a second ledger. Review is a specialized decision check with a minimum access contract and candidate-specific closure, not an inner-loop ceremony. No new runtime file, runner, schema, fixed root filename, or capture infrastructure is introduced.

Three designs were compared by source-level reasoning in the same authoring context, not independent experiments:

| Design | Strength | Reason for disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frozen control framework | Visible classifications, rigid forms, universal capture, critic gate. | Rejected: freezes incomplete understanding, imposes infrastructure regardless of the question, and relocates scope authority to the critic. |
| Bare build-inspect-fix loop | Little procedural overhead. | Insufficient for sustained Dreams: does not itself preserve artifact roles, required evidence, authority, allowance, or recovery across sessions. |
| Working agreement plus selective independent review | Keeps creative progress central while making consequential claims inspectable. | Chosen, with explicit risk: builders can underestimate review impact or defer criticism. Early commitment triggers, shared-mechanism impact checks, original-intent challenge, and exact-candidate closure counter that risk without proving it eliminated. |

## Domain decisions

- A Rule is a role within binding qualities. Failure establishes importance; diagnosis establishes cause; the repair choice establishes scope. Foundation debt is only an evidence-supported representation or architectural limitation.
- Current candidate, best checkpoint, and accepted baseline are separate roles. Candidate identity includes relevant code, assets, and configuration. Readiness, evidence limits, and candidate-specific acceptance are distinct facts.
- A blocking finding names a binding quality or demonstrates an omission from the original agreed encounter. A newly desired feature is not a defect. Neither builder nor reviewer can change obligations by relabeling them.
- Human authority is reused, not inferred from silence. Required observations stay required unless explicitly waived; a known failed quality is not a missing-check waiver. Acceptance is not merge or release permission.

These definitions are in the skill's CONTEXT.md. Execution decisions live in SKILL.md, not in a parallel glossary procedure.

## Stress points kept visible

A local-looking change can affect shared camera, timing, rendering, assets, or input behavior. The review reference therefore makes mechanism impact, not patch size, determine evidence invalidation. Uncertain impact broadens checking.

A brief can be incomplete without being permission to invent a larger product. A material original-intent challenge needs its connection and observation stated; unresolved binding disagreement needs a focused independent check or human decision.

A concise record can still go stale. Update it at actual consequential decisions and stopping points, and verify the real artifact on resumption. Do not recreate old history merely to fill a form.

Selective review can still arrive too late. Qualify the hard state early, review before consequential commitment, and reserve effort that allows the result to change the work. These are design safeguards, not demonstrated performance guarantees.
