# Provenance and migration

Dream Graph is Drew's self-contained replacement for the separate Dream Loop workflow. This is a local workflow decision, not a claim that the upstream project is deprecated or endorses this adaptation.

## Sources and retained mechanisms

- [achimala/dream-loop](https://github.com/achimala/dream-loop/tree/9bddb901f7d071cfefdd21e264267c757177a9df), revision `9bddb901f7d071cfefdd21e264267c757177a9df`: concrete generated targets, existing-product continuity, implementation and live capture, fresh criticism, repeated improvement, serious asset work, and changing approach when incremental work stalls. Original MIT notice is preserved in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- Prior Dream Graph at [skills revision cbd91c8](https://github.com/Drew-Goddyn/skills/tree/cbd91c80ba25af3834735ba8f6a128e9a964f28a/skills/dream-graph): branching imagination, probes, selective retention, inheritance, and focused shared-capability work.
- [Visual Convergence](https://github.com/Drew-Goddyn/skills/blob/cbd91c80ba25af3834735ba8f6a128e9a964f28a/skills/visual-convergence/SKILL.md): matched comparisons, best-version preservation, different evidence for different claims, diagnostic questions, and bounded retain/revert decisions. Its supporting eye-project account describes mixed outcomes, not proven general convergence.
- The [worked example](references/worked-example.md) and its pinned Dreams records: a chosen encounter, real authored assets, and continued refinement of the experience beyond a promising image or functioning mechanism.

## Deliberate changes

Separate learning, saving a checkpoint, readiness, human acceptance, and publication. Put the mandatory realization loop and decision criteria in the entrypoint. Keep exploration optional when a direction is chosen. Make review, asset guidance, and diagnostics portable inside this skill.

Do not inherit the exact-pixel goal, universal numerical acceptance threshold, subscription-tier routing, provider ordering, or spending assumptions from upstream. Preserve concrete ambition and independent criticism while reviewing the experience across its required states. These changes are design choices, not measured performance improvements.

## Migration

Install or update `dream-graph` as one complete folder. It needs neither `dream-loop` nor `visual-convergence` installed beside it. Use Dream Graph alone for this workflow rather than running the old scoring and stopping instructions in parallel. Other standalone skills in this repository are unchanged; their historical attribution remains intact.

Existing projects can keep their target images, checkpoints, captures, and useful notes wherever they are. Resume a chosen Dream directly; no graph conversion, renamed workspace, or repeated imagination round is required. Record only relationships that help the next decision.

## Validation limit

The rebuild's structural checks and decision-scenario review are documented in the repository's `dev/dream-graph/VALIDATION.md`. They are not a fresh-agent runtime trial. Use a bounded real creation with the target agent and observation tools to test whether the instructions sustain useful refinement without scope growth or endless review. Do not describe this version as behaviorally proven until that test exists.
