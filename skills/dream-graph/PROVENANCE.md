# Provenance and migration

Dream Graph is Drew's self-contained replacement for the separate Dream Loop workflow. This is a local workflow decision, not a claim that the upstream project is deprecated or endorses this adaptation.

Original MIT notice is preserved in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

Its supporting eye-project account describes mixed outcomes, not proven general convergence.
- The [worked example](references/worked-example.md) and its pinned Dreams records: a chosen encounter, real authored assets, and continued refinement beyond a promising image or functioning mechanism. Its new illustrative state section is explicitly separate from that history.

## Revision history and evidence levels

The original self-contained rebuild separated learning, checkpoints, readiness, acceptance, and publication; kept chosen-direction entry; and brought review and asset guidance into one portable skill. It deliberately omitted upstream's exact-pixel goal, universal numerical threshold, subscription routing, provider ordering, and spending assumptions.

[PR #4](https://github.com/Drew-Goddyn/skills/pull/4), merged October 12, 2026 at `2dbbaff4462a779e06e55d9dede2c8fbcd7a6bbc`, subsequently added Rules, foundation debt, and three-timescale motion review. Its author reported a Scooper rebuild, an earlier-version unattended observation stall, and CLI discovery. These are author-reported history, not independently inspected runtime evidence for this revision. The older structural validation account predates that policy layer.

The October 14, 2026 revision uses the three owner-supplied reviews and their October 13 canonical editorial adjudication as design input. It separates binding failure from causal diagnosis; organizes effort around useful creative or diagnostic moves; persists discoverable operational state; selects medium-appropriate targets and observation; and moves independent review to consequential decisions with exact-candidate closure. It preserves the existing readiness, required-human-check, acceptance, and repository-authority promises. The detailed disposition is authoring material in the repository's `dev/dream-graph/REVISION.md`, outside the execution bundle.

These are coordinated design choices, not measured improvements. No detailed `foundation-reset` procedure was imported; that companion remains optional and explicit-only.

## Migration

Install or update `dream-graph` as one complete folder. It needs neither `dream-loop` nor `visual-convergence` installed beside it. Use this workflow rather than combining it with their old scoring or stopping instructions. Other standalone skills are unchanged.

Existing Dreams continue rather than restart. Keep targets, assets, checkpoints, captures, and useful notes where they are. At the next normal resumption, make the active record discoverable, connect existing evidence, and fill only missing facts needed for the next decision. Distinguish current candidate, best checkpoint, and accepted baseline; mark unknown historical acceptance explicitly. No renamed workspace, graph conversion, replay of old work, or new concept audition is required.

Preserve old foundation-debt notes as history. Reassess their present cause and repair implication when relevant; an old label alone is not an architectural diagnosis. Reclassification cannot conceal an unresolved binding quality or promote unfinished work into the accepted baseline. Material changes to obligations or check policy still need their corresponding authority.

## Validation limit

The repository's `dev/dream-graph/VALIDATION.md` distinguishes historical claims, this revision's structural checks and author-performed decision walkthroughs, and untested behavior. The illustrative example teaches transitions; it is not a runtime result.

Learn during the next substantive Dream the human already wants: whether the encounter improves, important gains survive, useful effort reaches inspection and repair, and resumption preserves honest state. This revision commissions no additional Dream, absent-human reenactment, or matched benchmark as an adoption prerequisite. A successful future task would support that result, not establish general superiority or identify each mechanism's causal contribution.
