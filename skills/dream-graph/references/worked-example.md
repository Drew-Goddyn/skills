# A Small Visitor: source-grounded example

This illustrates the process using published Dreams records. It is not a run of the rebuilt skill, a complete reconstruction of the original session, or a claim that every part of the encounter passed.

## From existing experience to chosen direction

Kip already moved, recovered from falls, carried a plant, and disturbed leaves. The selected addition was a small bird that could approach and rest on his hat. Familiar movement could become something to attempt gently because another creature was nearby.

The saved builder brief explicitly closed concept selection. It supplied a generated target, preserved Kip and the garden, required the established asset-authoring process, and asked a fresh critic to inspect approach, response, perching, interruption, and re-approach. A cute standing-perch frame was insufficient.

## Why the first apparent success was not the finish

The chapter records an encounter whose returned bird could disappear among leaves or behind Kip. The bird's return existed, but the experience was hard to read. A focused follow-up gave it a visible place to land without redesigning the garden or enlarging the bird. The recorded follow-up also included a careful step and turn while perched and another approach without reset.

Under this skill's decision language, a promising implementation with an unreadable return calls for CONTINUE, not READY. The useful checkpoint is saved while the defining presentation gap is repaired. This is an interpretation of the records, not a historical verdict quoted from the original agents.

## What can be inherited honestly

The published account describes an accepted staging follow-up but retains limits: sampled-frame review, silent evidence, an earlier unremeasured stall, and known issues involving the pot, ledge recovery, and leaves. The records do not independently establish hands-on enjoyment or audio quality.

A compact graph could connect the existing movement, carrying, and leaves to the selected visitor, then the follow-up's readable return. A later idea may build on that demonstrated encounter while carrying the known limits forward. Any proposed next interaction is a new possibility, not an already validated capability.

The same distinction appeared in the first Kip build: visual reviews reached their target while movement reviews still rejected the result. That supports separate appearance and behavior checks; it does not establish that this rewritten skill reliably produces better work.

## Illustrative brief, active record, and transitions

Everything in this section is invented to demonstrate the revised policy, not additional history or a successful runtime trial. Paths, snapshots, observations, and reviews below are illustrative; they are not mandatory filenames or artifacts supplied with the skill.

An ordinary project `README.md` points to the active section of `story/visitor/notes.md`. Its brief at `story/visitor/brief.md` describes a quiet approach, perch, interruption, and readable return, using a saved visual reference and a short turn-and-return motion probe.

| Binding item | Source and required observation |
| --- | --- |
| Q1: the returning visitor is readable from the ordinary camera | Stated encounter; inspect the rendered approach, interruption, and return against the target. |
| Q2: perched feet keep their contact relationship through a gentle turn (Rule) | Stated behavior; observe the rendered turn and relevant contact states, not just position logs. |
| P1: existing movement and recovery survive | Protected accepted baseline; ordinary input and affected regression checks. |
| H1: control response still feels responsive while perched | Stated quality; a human input check is required, with no waiver. |

An initial review of `visitor-c1` finds detached feet under Q2. A discriminating check isolates one wrong local transform. This is a defining defect with a local cause, not foundation debt. Within the remaining allowance, the builder chooses a local correction and a closing observation of contact through the turn, then obtains a scoped independent follow-up on `visitor-c2`, including affected movement checks.

The active record now reads:

```text
Agreement: story/visitor/brief.md (Q1, Q2, P1, H1); references linked there.
Authority: direction selected; in-scope repair allowed; no quality/check waiver.
           Human owns H1 observation and final acceptance; no merge permission.
Current candidate: snapshots/visitor-c2 (code, assets, and configuration saved).
Best checkpoint: visitor-c2.
Accepted baseline: garden-c0; no visitor candidate accepted yet.
Evidence: review R2 on visitor-c2 closes Q2 and rechecks affected P1;
          Q1 evidence is explicitly carried forward as unaffected.
Open checks: H1 awaits human observation. No useful independent repair remains.
Decision: PAUSE pending H1. Acceptance: pending for visitor-c2.
Allowance: handoff remains; further implementation needs renewed allowance.
Next action: obtain H1 observation on visitor-c2, then decide readiness.
```

A lightweight graph node links rather than copies that state:

```text
Node: visitor-contact
Parents: garden-movement, visitor-first-encounter
Question: can the quiet perch survive a gentle turn?
Evidence: snapshots/visitor-c2; review R2 in story/visitor/notes.md
Decision at this node: PAUSE, required human observation pending
Next possibility: contact supports an encounter through ordinary movement;
                  further carrying or nesting behavior is not yet demonstrated.
Current state: story/visitor/notes.md, active section
```

If the human subsequently observes H1 on unchanged `visitor-c2` and reports it met, record that evidence. With all required checks satisfied and material objections closed, the same candidate becomes **READY; acceptance pending**. The observation is not itself a final acceptance or integration decision, and `garden-c0` stays the accepted baseline.

If the human instead reports that the response is sluggish, reopen H1 with the felt complaint and its conditions. Keep the candidate **PAUSE** when no repair allowance remains; use **CONTINUE** only when useful repair is authorized and funded. A numerical proxy can guard the issue only if it faithfully represents the complaint. This is not REJECT of the visitor idea.

An explicit missing-observation waiver would permit **READY - H1 check waived; control feel unverified** only if the other readiness obligations were met. It would not erase a known H1 failure.
