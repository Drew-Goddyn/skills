# Review and handoffs

Read before the first consequential review. SKILL.md owns the review triggers and decision meanings; this reference owns reviewer access, observation, and candidate-specific closure. Use one capable reviewer by default.

## Supply access without supplying a verdict

Give a fresh-context reviewer pointers to the candidate and launch instructions; original intended experience and saved targets; binding criteria and protected baseline; best checkpoint; required observations and known access limits; and relevant user-reported symptoms. Include remaining effort when requesting a next-action recommendation. A task message or existing issue can satisfy this contract; a new form is unnecessary.

A compact handoff might be:

> Inspect snapshot `visitor-c2` using the launch instructions in the active record. Begin with the encounter and saved target, then assess brief items Q1-Q3 and protected movement. Compare with checkpoint `visitor-c1` and accepted baseline `garden-c0`. The user reports losing sight of the returning bird. Rendered sequence and input checks are required; the human control-feel observation is still pending. Recommend the next decision within the remaining focused repair and handoff allowance. Form your initial impression before reading previous findings linked in the record.

These names illustrate a handoff, not an actual run. Provide neutral facts about intentional changes, asset versions, and access constraints where needed for fair inspection. Exclude persuasive success claims and the builder's implementation defense.

A separate pass in the builder's conversation is self-review, not independence. When independent access is unavailable, disclose it, do useful permitted checks, and apply SKILL.md's missing-check policy. Repository access does not establish runtime access. Claim only inspection actually performed.

## Encounter first, then assess the agreement

Form an initial artifact-led impression using the intended experience and target, before reading implementation explanations or prior verdicts. Then read the full brief and protected baseline, so an intentional tradeoff is not mistaken for a defect. This is one review with two perspectives, not a second agent or report.

Inspect the complete small experience: the primary action, relevant aftermath, and an important interruption, variation, or repeat. Check binding cross-state Rules where their failures could appear. For a static deliverable, inspect the appearance without inventing interaction requirements.

Compare with both the saved target and best checkpoint; verify protected baseline gains independently of which checkpoint looks best. Hold relevant viewing size, state, camera, and capture settings steady, identifying intentional changes. Confirm that the intended code and assets actually loaded.

For appearance, inspect composition, scale, lighting, materials, and decisive details at intended viewing size. Use actual audio for listening claims, actual input for interaction claims, and matched measurements for performance claims. A flattering still, passing test, and convincing encounter are different evidence.

## Select observation by failure and timescale

Use the least costly observation capable of answering the consequential question. Check actual observer and tool capabilities rather than embedding a universal claim about what agents can watch. Build missing capture support only when a required question needs it, charging the same allowance.

| Question | Useful observation | Coverage limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A transient pop, snap, or asset swap | A bounded rendered sequence, with frame-level inspection when needed. Exhaustive scans suit consequential transient failures when feasible. | Sampled frames do not cover every transition. Frame differences need interpretation. |
| A contact point or persistent object drifts over seconds | Track identity and rendered relationships through motion, such as a bird's feet during a turn or a shadow under a moving object. | State-position logs diagnose internal behavior; they do not prove the rendered relationship. Correlate them with rendered evidence. |
| An outcome, recovery, or return unfolds over a longer interval | Observe the state sequence across the relevant duration; spaced captures can establish persistent outcomes. | Sparse captures cannot establish intervening motion, pacing, or control feel. |
| Suspected dropped frames or performance regression | Relevant render traces and matched measurements, alongside rendered observation. | Recorded timestamps alone may not represent display performance. |

Reuse a fixed camera, seed, replay, or capture rig when it preserves the question. A scripted path that bypasses the failing real interaction does not answer it. Rendered-behavior claims require rendered observation; internal-state claims can use logs or tests. Distinguish saved from viewed media, sampled frames from playback, and recordings from hands-on judgment.

Human-only qualities can include response and weight of controls, perceived smoothness on a real display, or pacing. Follow the brief's designated observer. Missing observation is unverified, not automatically failed. Continue independent useful work when available, but apply the required-check gate at readiness. Turn a reported felt problem into a regression check only when the check faithfully represents it; preserve the original complaint when it does not.

## Return findings that can be acted on

Report the candidate and evidence actually inspected, coverage limits, important gains and regressions, and findings separated into:

- **Contract defects:** a binding item or protected gain, observed symptom, and evidence that would close it.
- **Brief challenges:** an observed failure of the original intended encounter that the brief omitted or misstated, with the connection explained.
- **Optional opportunities:** new aspirations or refinements, nonblocking unless the human changes scope.

A serious brief challenge cannot be dismissed merely for lacking an identifier. A new feature wish cannot become binding merely because the reviewer calls it a gap. Keep symptoms separate from tentative causes; neither importance nor recurrence proves architectural failure.

Recommend CONTINUE, READY, REJECT, or PAUSE using SKILL.md and the available allowance. Identify the next action that could most improve the encounter or resolve its decisive uncertainty. A matches/close/far grid can aid a particular visual comparison; it cannot close a failed binding quality or substitute for whole-encounter judgment. Scores are not calibrated across reviewers.

## Close objections on the handoff candidate

Bind review evidence and findings to a recoverable candidate identity. A commit is insufficient when uncommitted code, external assets, or configuration determine the result. Record what was checked and under which relevant conditions.

After an edit, invalidate evidence the change could affect. Assess the mechanism touched, not just the diff's size: a small change to shared timing, camera, rendering, input handling, or assets can invalidate broad judgments. When impact is uncertain, broaden the check rather than silently inherit approval.

A local repair may use a scoped independent follow-up on the exact handoff candidate, with explicit links to still-applicable evidence for unaffected qualities. Preserve reviewer continuity without importing the builder's whole conversation. A representation or experience change needs correspondingly broader review. Another complete cold-start critique after every small edit is unnecessary.

Before READY, account for every material objection: demonstrated closure on the candidate, a scope-grounded explanation that the objection was an optional opportunity, or explicit human resolution. An unresolved material disagreement about a binding quality needs a focused independent check or human decision; the builder cannot relabel it away. A missing-check waiver does not dispose of a known failure.

A reviewer's decision word is a recommendation, not authority to enlarge or lower the commission. A historical favorable review is not transferable approval. Resolve disagreements by evidence and the agreement, not repeated reviews in search of approval.

## Hand over existing useful artifacts

Provide the runnable or inspectable candidate, the active-record locator, useful existing comparison or recording, findings and limits, and candidate-specific acceptance status. Link existing evidence rather than assembling a new dossier each round.

For a subsequent creative pass, point to the accepted baseline, demonstrated capabilities and limitations, representative captures, and the few graph relationships that change its possibilities. Keep rejected work as evidence, not a backlog.
