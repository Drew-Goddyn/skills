# Review and handoffs

Read this when coordinating separate contributors, claiming independent review, comparing a consequential Dream candidate, or preparing a milestone handoff.

## Lightweight roles

Use roles only when they create a real context or review boundary:

- **Dreamweaver** — owns the graph, preserves identity and inheritance, and chooses what kind of work is needed next.
- **Imagine contributor** — explores with selective context.
- **Dream builder** — gets deeper implementation context after a seed is chosen.
- **Reviewer** — judges the running artifact against the seed, retained best, and protected behavior.

One top-level agent may coordinate all of these. A second pass in the same context is self-review, not independent review.

## Review the artifact

Give the reviewer the Dream seed, retained best, candidate, required states, and evidence limits. Prefer observable symptoms before explanations of cause.

Keep these decisions separate:

- Did the candidate improve the intended experience?
- Did protected behavior survive?
- Is the candidate the new retained best?
- Has the human accepted the Dream?

Scores may support discussion but do not replace whole-experience judgment, and scores from different reviewers are not a calibrated series.

## Match evidence to the question

Use:

- stills for appearance;
- timed playback for motion;
- actual audio for sound;
- real input sequences and traces for interaction;
- matched performance runs for performance claims.

A passing short outing and a failing stress run can both be true. Preserve both. A new passing attempt does not erase an earlier failure, and one differently timed failure does not automatically establish causation.

## Human play

Ask the human to play when the decision is experiential, obvious in ordinary play, or impossible to settle from available evidence. Demonstrate the specific scenario first when possible.

Keep subtle implementation tradeoffs and repeated diagnostic chores out of the human loop unless their judgment is actually needed.

## Practical handoffs

Keep handoffs phase-specific:

- **Imagine** — graph snapshot, active lineages, decisive probes.
- **Dream seed** — pull, moment, invariants, unknown, evidence, inheritance.
- **Dream milestone** — playable location or exact revision plus one compact review package.
- **Retention** — preferred revision, inherited gains, remaining limits, parked lessons.

Lead with the decision and one useful comparison. Include exact source identity and restart information when relevant. Avoid copying the entire project history or every prior artifact into each handoff.

## Retention checks

Before retaining, watch for:

- **Score chasing** — a metric has replaced judgment of the whole experience.
- **Refinement drift** — polishing continues after the Dream's important question is already answered.
- **Unproven attribution** — a correlation is being reported as a demonstrated cause.
- **Evidence substitution** — tests or builder commentary are standing in for the actual behavior they do not observe.

Correct the claim or stop the branch rather than manufacturing more work.
