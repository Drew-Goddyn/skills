# Capability gyms

Use a [gym](../CONTEXT.md) only when a chosen Dream or repeated branches expose a shared capability worth strengthening separately. Keep the work in service of the selected encounter.

## Define a bounded improvement

Name the capability, observed failure, representative scenario, protected gains, and evidence needed for a useful improvement. Examples include carrying, locomotion, deformation, rendering, capture, conversion, or sound response.

Choose the smallest coherent improvement that serves the experience. Charge gym work to the Dream's remaining allowance in its active record unless the human explicitly commissions a separate allowance. Reuse that record; a gym creates neither a new budget nor a second accounting ledger. Reserve the real-deliverable check and handoff effort.

## Test what the capability must do

Use cheap deterministic checks for bulk iteration when faithful. Keep a real-deliverable check for timing, rendering, interaction, or perception claims. Preserve the failing mechanism and meaningful assertions rather than adding fixture-specific assistance or bypassing the interaction.

Preserve independently supported gains even when a larger attempt fails. State what was demonstrated, what remains unobserved, and where the capability still fails. Passing a capability test supports that claim, not every experience built on it.

## Return to the chosen experience

Integrate a validated gain only within authority, preserving its narrower status. Recheck the Dream in ordinary use and update affected evidence: capability success does not establish appearance, feel, or readiness.

Resume realization when the blockage clears. Otherwise PAUSE with the limitation and best recoverable work. Link a meaningful inherited capability in the graph without duplicating current state. The chosen experience determines whether further gym work is worth its cost.
