# Assets and diagnostics

Read before substantial visual asset or representation work, and revisit when important quality gaps persist.

## Choose for the result

Inspect the existing authoring pipeline and preserve useful source assets. Choose among permitted external assets, image-to-3D, Blender or another authoring tool, procedural construction, and combinations based on the required appearance and behavior. Procedural work is valid when it genuinely serves the result, not merely because primitives are convenient.

For an important object, derive or isolate its appearance from the selected target instead of quietly reimagining it. Use authored or generated textures, surface detail, and lighting inputs where they improve the intended look. Flat color may be the right art direction; a placeholder surface is not the same decision.

Use services and spending only within permission. Verify actual access before claiming a tool is unavailable. Provider names, model prices, and account tiers are not part of this skill's quality standard.

## Qualify the hard state early

Test an uncertain representation in the actual deliverable where it is most likely to fail: deformation during a fall, an interrupted transition, independent movement, close-up material response, or a difficult camera view. A convincing still does not qualify an interactive representation.

Keep the working baseline while testing alternatives. For imported or converted assets, compare source appearance with unchanged runtime appearance before combining conversion fixes with other changes. Separate an inadequate source from losses in conversion, shading, textures, or motion.

## Diagnose instead of decorating

When the same meaningful gap persists, name the observed problem and a plausible distinction that would change the next decision. Hold unrelated conditions fixed and verify the tested mechanism actually operates. For example, determine whether the weak result comes from geometry, material conversion, lighting, or staging before another compound tweak.

A focused test can be useful even when its candidate is rejected. Preserve the finding and its conditions without presenting it as a visual gain. Stop experiments that merely produce another version of the same unanswered question.

If the asset or approach cannot deliver the target, make a coherent replacement within scope and budget. Keep useful gains recoverable. Judge the rebuilt whole, allow rough intermediate stages, and verify the protected experience before replacing the checkpoint.
