# Assets and diagnostics

Read before substantial visual asset or representation work, and revisit when important quality gaps persist. Use the main loop's investment choice and repair branch; asset work draws on the same allowance.

## Choose for the result

Inspect the existing authoring pipeline and preserve useful source assets. Choose among permitted external assets, image-to-3D, Blender or another authoring tool, procedural construction, and combinations based on required appearance and behavior. Procedural work is valid when it serves the result, not merely because primitives are convenient.

For an important object, derive or isolate its appearance from the saved target and binding properties rather than quietly reimagining it. Use authored or generated textures, surface detail, and lighting inputs where they improve the intended look. Flat color may be the art direction; a placeholder surface is a different decision.

Use services and spending only within permission. Verify actual access before declaring a tool unavailable. Provider names, prices, and account tiers are not part of the quality standard.

## Qualify the hard state early

Identify the uncertainty most likely to make an expensive realization worthless. Test it in the actual deliverable: deformation during a fall, an interrupted transition, independent movement, close-up material response, or a difficult camera view. Give the test enough finish to judge the relevant appeal. A convincing still does not qualify an interactive representation.

Preserve the prior best and protected baseline while testing alternatives. For imported or converted assets, compare the source with unchanged runtime appearance before combining conversion fixes with other changes. Distinguish an inadequate source from losses in conversion, shading, textures, or motion.

Choose the simplest observation that answers the question. Reuse capture support where adequate rather than making a rig an automatic prerequisite to asset work.

## Diagnose instead of decorating

Name the observed problem and a plausible distinction that would change the next decision. Hold unrelated conditions fixed and verify that the tested mechanism actually operates. For example, distinguish geometry, material conversion, lighting, and staging before another compound tweak.

A local defect can violate a binding quality without implicating the representation. Repeated failures trigger diagnosis under the main repair branch, not a predetermined rebuild. A focused test is useful when it changes the explanation or next action, even if its candidate is rejected; preserve its conditions without presenting it as a visual gain.

When evidence shows the approach cannot deliver the target, qualify a coherent replacement within scope and remaining allowance. Keep useful work recoverable, allow rough intermediate stages, and check both the rebuilt encounter and protected gains before retaining it. End attempts that only reproduce the same unanswered question.
